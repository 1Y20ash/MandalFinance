Refund & Cancellation Policy

How donation cancellations, payment failures and refund requests are handled.

1. Donation cancellations

A donation that has not been successfully captured by the payment gateway is not treated as a completed donation. If a payment attempt fails, is cancelled or expires before capture, no successful donation receipt is issued for that attempt.

2. Refund requests

If a successfully completed donation needs to be refunded, the donor should contact the Mandal administrator through the established administrative channel and provide the donation or receipt reference, transaction details and the reason for the request.

3. Review and processing

Refund requests are reviewed by the Mandal based on the donation record, payment status and applicable requirements. Where a refund is approved, it is processed through the payment gateway and, where supported, returned to the original payment method.

4. Duplicate or erroneous payments

Duplicate charges or payments made in error should be reported with the relevant transaction or receipt information so that the Mandal can verify the payment and determine the appropriate resolution.

5. Payment-provider processing

Payment authorization, capture and refund execution depend on the configured payment gateway and the payment method used. A payment showing as pending is not necessarily a completed donation.

6. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the donation process or applicable requirements change. The current version is the one published on this page.
