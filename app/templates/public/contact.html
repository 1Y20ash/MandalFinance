Donation enquiries

For donation receipts, payment issues, duplicate payments, refund requests or corrections, please contact the Mandal administrator through the established administrative channel and provide your donation or transaction reference.

What to include in a payment enquiry

Please include the donation or receipt number, payment transaction reference, approximate payment date and the issue you are reporting. Do not send card numbers, UPI PINs, passwords, OTPs or other payment credentials.

Payment credentials are handled by the payment gateway. The Mandal will never ask you to disclose an OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN or password to resolve a donation issue.
