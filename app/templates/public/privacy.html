Privacy Notice

Last updated: 9 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: name, username, email, phone number and authentication data needed to operate an approved account.
  • Donation data: donor name and, where provided, phone number and email address, donation amount, purpose, payment references and receipt information.
  • Financial records: transaction references and supporting documents required to maintain the Mandal's financial records.
  • Security/audit data: limited technical information such as request IP address and browser information when needed for security, audit and incident investigation.

Why we use it

We use personal data only for identified purposes such as account administration, donation processing, issuing receipts, maintaining financial records, protecting the service, preventing fraud or misuse, responding to requests, and meeting applicable legal or accounting obligations.

Payment processing

Online donations are processed through the configured payment gateway. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by this application. We retain only the payment identifiers and transaction information required for verification, reconciliation, receipts and financial records.

Documents and security

Uploaded financial documents are stored in private storage and are intended to be accessible only to authorised users. Security controls include authentication, authorization, encrypted transport, integrity checks, access controls and audit logging.

Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate financial/accounting requirements, security investigation, dispute handling or another applicable legal requirement. Data that is no longer required should be securely deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law and verification requirements, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion where retention is no longer required, information about processing, and withdrawal of consent where consent is the applicable basis.

For a privacy or data-rights request, contact the Mandal administrator through the official contact channel provided by the Mandal. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other authentication secrets in a privacy request.

Changes

This notice may be updated when the application's processing activities, security controls or applicable legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated through the service.

This notice is an engineering privacy notice for MandalFinance and is not legal advice or a legal certification of compliance.
