MandalFinance Privacy Notice — version {{ notice_version }}

Who we are

MandalFinance is a financial management system operated for the Ganesh Mandal. The Mandal acts as the organisation responsible for the personal data processed through this application.

Personal data we process

  • Account information such as name, username, email address and optional phone number.
  • Authentication information, including password hashes and security/session records.
  • Financial information connected to members, donors, contributions, expenses or payments where required for the service.
  • Documents and evidence uploaded for authorised financial operations.
  • Security and audit information such as request metadata needed to protect the service and investigate incidents.

Why we process it

We use personal data only as needed to provide account access, manage Mandal finances, process and reconcile payments, maintain financial records, secure the service, maintain auditability, respond to privacy requests and meet applicable legal or operational obligations.

Optional analytics and non-essential communications require a separate affirmative preference. They are not a condition of using the core financial service.

Payments and service providers

Payment processing may involve Razorpay. Cloud infrastructure, private storage, database hosting, Redis/rate-limit infrastructure, monitoring or email services may also process limited information where required. Only information necessary for the relevant purpose should be shared.

Security

MandalFinance applies access control, authenticated sessions, CSRF protection, rate limiting, audit logging, protected document storage, payment verification and other technical and organisational safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security, so suspected incidents are investigated through the incident-response process.

Retention and deletion

Personal data is retained only for the period necessary for the stated purpose and applicable financial, legal, security or audit requirements. Requests to delete applicable personal data are reviewed so that mandatory financial and audit records are not destroyed improperly.

Your privacy choices and rights

Authenticated users can use the Privacy Center to request access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or submit a privacy grievance. Requests follow a controlled review workflow and may require identity verification.

Contact and grievance

Privacy requests and grievances should be submitted through the Privacy Center when an account is available. The Mandal should maintain an operational privacy contact and grievance mechanism outside the application as required by its governance process.
