Privacy Notice

MandalFinance Privacy Notice — version {{ notice_version }} | Effective: 9 September 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data?

MandalFinance is operated for the Ganesh Mandal. For personal data processed through this application, the Mandal is the organisation responsible for deciding the purposes and means of processing.

Privacy and grievance contact: the Mandal's designated privacy/grievance contact. The Mandal should publish and maintain the current contact details wherever this notice is displayed.

2. What personal data do we collect?

  • Account data: full name, username, email address and, where needed, optional phone number.
  • Security data: password hash, authentication/session records and security metadata.
  • Financial data: donation or contribution details, transaction references and payment identifiers required for accounting, reconciliation or investigation.
  • Documents: files and document metadata uploaded as evidence for authorised financial operations.
  • Operational/security metadata: limited request, IP-address and user-agent information where required for security, abuse prevention and incident investigation.

We apply data minimisation. The standard offline donation workflow does not require donor PAN, postal address or donor email. Optional information should only be supplied where the stated purpose requires it.

3. Why do we use personal data?

  • create and secure accounts and provide authorised access;
  • record and reconcile Mandal income, donations, contributions and expenses;
  • process and verify payments and issue or maintain financial records and receipts;
  • store authorised financial evidence and maintain accountability;
  • prevent abuse, investigate security incidents and maintain auditability;
  • receive and process privacy requests and grievances; and
  • comply with applicable legal, accounting, tax, security or governance obligations.

4. Basis and necessity

Core processing is limited to information necessary to provide the requested financial-management service, maintain security and accountability, or satisfy applicable obligations. Where consent is the appropriate basis for an optional purpose, consent is requested separately, recorded with its notice version and source, and can be withdrawn.

Optional analytics and non-essential communications are not required to use the core financial service.

5. Who may receive personal data?

Access is limited according to role and operational need. Depending on the feature used, limited information may be processed by service providers supporting:

  • Payment processing: Razorpay, when a payment feature uses that provider.
  • Database/private file storage: the configured PostgreSQL and private storage infrastructure used by the application.
  • Rate limiting/security infrastructure: Redis or equivalent infrastructure configured for production security controls.
  • Monitoring or communications: only providers actually enabled by the Mandal, and only for the relevant purpose.

The Mandal should keep the processor register current and must not represent an optional provider as active unless it is actually configured and used.

6. Payment information

Where online payments are enabled, payment processing is performed through the configured payment provider. MandalFinance verifies server-side payment responses and records only the payment information needed for reconciliation, financial records, security and dispute/investigation purposes. Payment credentials such as card PINs, CVVs or banking passwords are not collected by MandalFinance.

7. Documents and storage

Uploaded financial evidence is intended for authorised users and stored through the application's protected storage controls. Documents should not be uploaded unless they are relevant to the financial operation. Access and deletion are subject to authorisation and the applicable retention/deletion procedure.

8. Retention

We retain each category of personal data only for as long as necessary for its stated purpose and applicable financial, legal, security or audit requirements. Detailed retention periods are maintained in the MandalFinance Retention Schedule. Deletion requests are reviewed so that mandatory financial or audit records are not improperly destroyed.

9. Security

Security controls include authenticated sessions, role-based access control, CSRF protection, rate limiting, protected document storage, payment verification, audit logging and secure production configuration. Access to personal data is restricted according to role and need. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and Data Principal rights

Authenticated users can use the Privacy Center to request access, correction, deletion of applicable personal data, withdrawal of optional consent, or to submit a privacy grievance. Requests follow the documented workflow and may require identity verification before disclosure or action.

Withdrawing optional consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal. Where processing is necessary for the core service or a legal/accounting obligation, withdrawal of optional consent does not require that necessary processing to stop.

11. Consent and optional processing

Optional analytics and communications are separate purposes. They are not bundled with core service access. Consent records identify the purpose, notice version, source and grant/withdrawal time. A user can change these preferences through the Privacy Center.

12. Grievances and contact

Privacy questions, requests and grievances can be submitted through the Privacy Center for authenticated users. The Mandal must also maintain an accessible grievance/contact channel and current designated contact details outside the application. Requests are handled according to the Data Principal Rights Procedure and applicable requirements.

13. Notice changes

This notice is versioned. Material changes to purposes, categories, processors or rights handling should result in an updated notice version and, where required, renewed or updated consent for optional processing.

Last reviewed: 9 September 2026.
