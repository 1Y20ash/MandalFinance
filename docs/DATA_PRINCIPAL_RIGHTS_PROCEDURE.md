# Data Principal Rights Procedure

## Supported requests

- Access to personal data held by MandalFinance.
- Correction of inaccurate account data.
- Deletion of applicable personal data, subject to mandatory financial/legal retention.
- Withdrawal of optional consent.
- Privacy grievances.

## Workflow

`REQUESTED → IDENTITY VERIFIED → REVIEWED → PROCESSED → COMPLETED`

A request may be rejected only for a documented reason. The requester must be informed of the outcome through the operational contact mechanism maintained by the Mandal.

## Identity verification

Authenticated account requests begin with the existing authenticated session. Higher-risk requests, particularly deletion or requests made through an external channel, require an appropriate additional identity-verification procedure before disclosure or destructive action. Verification evidence must not be stored unnecessarily.

## Access response

Access responses should contain only the requesting person's data and should exclude passwords, secrets, private storage credentials, unrelated users' records and unnecessary internal security data.

## Correction

Users can correct supported profile fields from the Privacy Center. Financial history must not be silently rewritten as a side effect of profile correction.

## Deletion

Deletion requires a review of financial, audit and legal retention requirements. Unnecessary account data and applicable documents should be removed; records that must remain for financial integrity should be retained with personal linkage minimised/anonymised where technically and legally appropriate.

## Grievances

Privacy grievances are recorded as privacy requests and must be reviewed by the designated privacy owner within the organisation's documented grievance process.
